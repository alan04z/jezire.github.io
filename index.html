About Jezira Alkhair

Jezira Alkhair General Trading is a trusted international trading company based in Zakho, Iraq. We specialize in importing and exporting quality food products, biscuits, sweets, candy, snacks, and other premium goods from international markets.

Our business connects Iraq with trusted suppliers and manufacturers across Turkey, Mexico, China, Egypt, Greece, Ukraine, and Italy. We focus on quality, reliability, strong business relationships, and excellent service.
